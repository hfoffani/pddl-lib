An airline must give ten planes (p1 to p10) their routine check during the
next four days. There is one maintenance crew. Each day the crew can work at
one airport (Madrid, Paris or Rome), or take the day off. When the crew works
at an airport, every plane parked there that day gets its check.

Where each plane is parked:

| plane | day 1 | day 2 | day 3 | day 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| p1 | Madrid | Madrid | Madrid | Madrid |
| p2 | Madrid | Madrid | Madrid | Paris |
| p3 | Madrid | Madrid | Rome | Rome |
| p4 | Paris | Madrid | Rome | Madrid |
| p5 | Paris | Madrid | Rome | Paris |
| p6 | Madrid | Paris | Paris | Rome |
| p7 | Madrid | Paris | Paris | Madrid |
| p8 | Madrid | Rome | Paris | Paris |
| p9 | Rome | Rome | Paris | Rome |
| p10 | Rome | Rome | Paris | Madrid |

Every working day is expensive. Which days should the crew work, and where,
so that all ten planes get checked with as few working days as possible?
