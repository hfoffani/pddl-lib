A robot arm rearranges eight toy blocks, labeled a to h, on a table.

Right now there are three stacks:
- a is on the table, c is on a, e is on c, and g is on top of e.
- b is on the table and d is on b.
- f is on the table and h is on f.

The arm holds at most one block at a time and can only take a block that has
nothing on top of it. Each of these counts as one move:
- pick up a block from the table,
- take a block off the top of another block,
- put the held block down on the table,
- put the held block on top of a block that has nothing on it.

The goal is two towers: b on a, c on b, and d on c; and g on h, f on g, and e
on f. (Where a and h stand doesn't matter.)

What is the shortest sequence of moves that does it? List every move.
